WHAT I WANT TO ADD: 
Revenue Recovery Agent: Feature List
1. Detect (revenue at risk)
Ingest one batch of ~300-500 at-risk items across four leak types: failed payments, abandoned checkouts, failed subscription renewals/mandates, and overdue B2B invoices.
Show a Revenue-at-Risk board with amount, leak type, days at risk, and recovery probability for each item.
Detect payment degradation by watching success rate per bank, UPI app, card network and gateway route. Flag a drop (e.g. “HDFC cards down 14 pts in 2 hours”) before it shows up in monthly numbers.
Separate a systemic failure (route or issuer problem) from an individual failure (customer issue).
2. Diagnose (root cause)
Classify each failure by its decline code into a cause bucket: insufficient funds, issuer/bank down, OTP/3DS failure, expired card, mandate limit exceeded, UPI timeout, fraud block, hard decline, or customer disputing the charge.
For abandoned checkouts, tag the drop-off step: payment page, OTP, coupon, shipping cost, or no intent.
For receivables, tag the reason: forgot, cash-flow delay, invoice dispute, PO mismatch, or wrong contact.
Attach a confidence score and evidence to every diagnosis, and route low-confidence cases to a human.
3. Decide (intervention engine)
Map each cause to the cheapest effective action:
Bank down: retry later or reroute through another gateway.
Insufficient funds: retry after payday or salary-credit timing.
Expired card: send a card-update link.
UPI mandate failure: run a mandate retry sequence.
Abandoned cart: send a reminder, then a small incentive.
Overdue invoice: send a polite nudge, then a firm reminder, then a call, then escalation.
Run a cost-aware decision that compares expected recovery against the cost of the action (SMS, WhatsApp, voice call, discount) and skips low-value cases.
Choose the channel using customer history, preferred language and best contact time.
Cap discounts and incentives with a margin guardrail, so the agent never gives away more than the cap.
4. Execute (bounded workflows)
Smart retry sequencer that times retries to the failure type and respects the retry limits of card networks and UPI.
Mandate retry sequencer that respects pre-debit notification windows and per-mandate limits.
Omnichannel outreach over WhatsApp, SMS, email and voice, with personalised, auditable message templates.
Hinglish voice recovery agent:
Simulated call transcripts that switch naturally between Hindi and English.
Handles objections such as “salary aane do” (“let my salary come in”), “already paid” and “send me the link”.
Sends a payment link by WhatsApp during the call.
Detects intent: pay now, promise later, dispute, or hostile.
Promise-to-pay tracker:
Captures the promised date and amount.
Sends reminders before the date.
Marks the promise kept or broken.
Re-routes on a broken promise, and gives repeat promise-breakers lower priority.
Dispute handling: when a customer says “I already paid” or “invoice is wrong”, the agent freezes outreach and opens a reconciliation task instead of chasing.
5. Stopping rules (hard and visible)
Max touches per customer per week, and max per case in total.
Stop on payment, dispute, opt-out/DND, bereavement or hardship flag, or a legal hold.
No contact outside permitted hours (e.g. 8 AM-7 PM for collections calls).
Stop when retry success probability drops below a threshold, or when the cost of recovery exceeds the amount at stake.
Stop on a tone or sentiment trigger, such as anger or distress, and hand over to a human.
Every stop shows the rule that fired on the case.
6. Compliance and escalation ladder
Consent and opt-out check before every outbound message or call.
DND and template-registration checks for SMS and WhatsApp.
Regulator-aligned contact windows and frequency caps (RBI recovery norms, TRAI rules, DPDP consent, as applicable).
Collections-safe language checks that block threats, public shaming, third-party disclosure and misleading urgency.
Escalation ladder: automated nudge, then a firmer reminder, then an agent voice call, then a human collector, then a manager/legal review. Each step requires evidence that the previous step happened.
Human-approval queue for high-value cases, discounts above the cap, disputes and legal steps.
7. Audit trail
Immutable, timestamped log of each decision: input data, diagnosis, rule or policy applied, action taken, and outcome.
A “Why did the agent do this?” panel per case with the full reasoning chain.
Message and call transcripts stored with consent flags.
Exportable audit report (CSV/PDF) for compliance review.
Replay mode that steps through one case from detection to closure.
8. Measured recovery (the proof)
Holdout control group (e.g. 15-20% of cases receive no intervention) to measure incremental recovery, not just the cases that would have paid anyway.
Headline result: “₹X recovered out of ₹Y at risk, with Z% lift over control, at a cost of ₹C.”
Breakdown by leak type, cause, channel and intervention.
Net recovery after discounts and outreach cost, plus ROI.
Before/after comparison against a baseline “blast reminder to everyone” strategy.
Time-to-recover, recovery rate by day, and a funnel from at-risk to contacted to promised to recovered.
Confidence intervals or a basic significance note, so the lift isn’t just a big number.
9. Operator dashboard
Live batch run button with a simulated timeline of actions as they execute.
Case-level drill-down with the timeline, diagnosis, actions and audit entries.
Policy panel with adjustable levers (max retries, discount cap, quiet hours, minimum case value) that re-run the batch and show the recovery change.
Alerts view for systemic issues, such as a gateway or bank degradation.
Human-review queue with approve, reject and edit.
10. Failure handling and safety
Idempotency, so the same payment is never retried twice or double-charged.
Duplicate-contact prevention across channels.
Graceful fallback when a channel fails, such as a WhatsApp delivery failure falling back to SMS.
Guardrails against hallucinated amounts: the agent only quotes figures pulled from the ledger.
Sensitive-data masking (card numbers, phone numbers) in logs and screens.
11. Differentiators for the judges
The incremental lift vs holdout number, not just “recovered ₹”.
Visible stopping rules and a “cases the agent chose NOT to chase” list, with reasons.
Hinglish voice transcript demo with a payment link sent mid-call.
Systemic root-cause detection (gateway/bank degradation) plus per-customer recovery in one flow.
Policy sliders that change the outcome live.
A one-page Recovery Report with the headline money number, cost, lift and compliance summary
